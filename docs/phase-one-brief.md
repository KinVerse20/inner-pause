# Inner Pause - Phase 1 Brief

21 November 2026 | Requirements summary for review

## 1. Home: choose what you need

Recreate the Home screen in SS 1, using its cream background, rounded cards, icons and central Pause button.

- Show six main options: Sleep, Reset, Focus, Confidence, Calm and Release.
- Tapping an option opens its curated music directly in the player.
- Below the player, show "Why this Pause?" explaining the selected frequencies and the intention behind that session.
- Keep the frequency-editing option available during playback.

## 2. "What's on your mind?": personal journal

- Place the journal section below the main Pause options.
- Users write a personal entry and save it for later.
- Journal content does not trigger emotion analysis, chakra mapping or music recommendations.
- Replace the screenshot's "We'll help you find the right Pause" wording with personal-journal wording. Suggested copy: "Your space to reflect. Write what's on your mind and save it in your personal journal."
- Do not add an "analysis disabled" message to the interface.
- The reference also shows a Speak button; whether this saves a voice note or transcribes speech remains to be decided.

## 3. Create your own sound session

Add a separate option on Home for creating a personal sound session.

Flow: Select emotions > map them to chakras > combine repeated chakras > assign frequencies and sounds > open the music player.

- Use the emotions explicitly selected by the user, not their journal entry.
- Carry forward the agreed session options: background sound, 5/10/15 minutes per frequency, sequential playback and adjustable undertone strength.
- This is a single custom sound session in Phase 1; the curated Journeys feature belongs to Phase 2.

## 4. "For the big moments in your life"

- Add the Home entry and dedicated Moments screen shown in SS 1.
- Organize moments under Coming up, Happening now and Just happened.
- Offer approximately 15-20 curated situations. Examples include a big event coming up, an upcoming interview, a difficult conversation, just having had a fight and anxiety about the future.
- Selecting a specific moment opens its curated music directly.
- Include "Why this Pause?" for each moment, explaining that session's intention and frequency choices.
- The final moment list and its sound/frequency assignments still need to be defined.

## 5. Music player and visual references

- Use the longer blue-background video as the player reference: deep-blue background, animated layered rings around the Pause mark, session title, chakra/frequency label, timer and playback controls.
- Include the expandable "Why this Pause?" section below the player controls.
- Retain frequency-editing controls alongside the reference design. Confirm whether this means changing the Hz value, undertone strength, or both; these are different controls.
- The video also shows gentle prompts and end-of-session feedback: Better, Same, Not better, or Skip.
- Use the short cream-background video as a reference for the animated brand mark. It shows an "I'm ready" screen, but the requested Home and Moment shortcuts should open playback directly without that extra step.

## 6. About Us: The Harmony Form

Recreate the content and visual language of SS 2, including the logo, primary and standalone marks, wordmark, central Pause-button treatment and app-icon examples.

Include all of its main sections:

- The story: "When we pause, we return to harmony."
- What it means: Layers of sound, The pause, Resonance and Center stillness.
- The experience in one look: Life gets noisy > You pause > You return to yourself > You move forward with clarity.
- The essence: "Sound meets stillness. Pause creates harmony. You come home to you."
- Brand message: "Pause the noise. Return to you."

Suggested structure, not yet finalized: three short pages or sections - Our Story; The Meaning of the Mark; The Experience and Essence. Together they should preserve all the reference content.

## Scope and next decisions

Phase 1 covers Home shortcuts, personal journaling, emotion-selected sound sessions, curated Moments, the player and About Us. Phase 2 will cover curated Journeys and will be discussed separately.

Before implementation, settle the frequency control, voice-journal behavior, final Moments catalog and About Us page split. This document records the requested direction only; no app changes or deployment are included in this step. Future work remains separate from the live app until approved for release.
